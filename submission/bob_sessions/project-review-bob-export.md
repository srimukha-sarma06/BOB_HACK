## How to Export Your Bob Session

1. Complete all 4 Bob mission prompts in IBM Bob IDE
2. Export your task/session history from Bob
3. Save the exported file here as: `exported-bob-task-history-{date}.md`
4. Optional: Add a screenshot of token consumption as: `exported-bob-task-history-{date}-consumption.png`
